CORPORATE WORKPLACE POLICY (2023 EDITION)

1. REMOTE WORK POLICY
Employees are permitted to work remotely up to 3 days per week upon manager approval. Core office days are mandatory on Tuesdays and Thursdays.

2. LEARNING & DEVELOPMENT STIPEND
Each full-time employee is eligible for an annual professional learning stipend of $1,500 USD for courses, certifications, and conferences.

3. WORKING HOURS
Standard office core working hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM local time. Flexible start times are available between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM.
