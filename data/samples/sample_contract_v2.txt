1. PAYMENT TERMS & SCHEDULE
The Client agrees to remit full payment for all invoices within 15 days of the invoice date. Late payments shall incur a interest penalty rate of 2.5% per month.

All purchases and service fees are strictly non-refundable upon contract execution. No cash refunds or service credits will be issued under any circumstances.

The total aggregate liability of the Provider under this Agreement shall be capped at $100,000 USD or the total fees paid in the preceding 12 months.

4. TERMINATION NOTICE
Either party may terminate this agreement without cause by providing at least 60 days written notice to the other party.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY
All proprietary software code and client data shall remain confidential in perpetuity following agreement termination.
