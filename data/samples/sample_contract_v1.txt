MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT (VERSION 1.0)

1. PAYMENT TERMS & SCHEDULE
The Client agrees to remit full payment for all invoices within 30 days of the invoice date. Late payments shall incur a interest penalty rate of 1.5% per month.

2. REFUND & CANCELLATION POLICY
Clients are eligible for a 100% full refund if cancellation notice is provided in writing within 14 days of contract execution. No refunds will be provided after 14 days.

3. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
The total aggregate liability of the Provider under this Agreement shall be strictly capped at $50,000 USD or the total fees paid in the preceding 6 months.

4. TERMINATION NOTICE
Either party may terminate this agreement without cause by providing at least 30 days written notice to the other party.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY
All proprietary software code and client data shall remain confidential for a period of 3 years following agreement termination.
