CORPORATE WORKPLACE POLICY (2024 UPDATE)

1. REMOTE WORK POLICY
Employees are permitted to work remotely up to 1 day per week upon director approval. Core office presence is required 4 days per week.

2. LEARNING & DEVELOPMENT STIPEND
Each full-time employee is eligible for an annual professional learning stipend of $2,500 USD for courses, certifications, and conferences.

3. WORKING HOURS
Standard office core working hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time. All team meetings must occur within core hours.
